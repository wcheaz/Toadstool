# Decision Log — Direct Trading Platform

**Audience:** delivery team, Programme Sponsor, Risk & Compliance. Records decisions, the assumptions behind
them and what changed from the original plan ([`project-plan.md`](../project-plan.md)), so that a reviewer can
see why the platform is built the way it is. Add new entries at the bottom; do not rewrite old ones —
supersede them.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why | Alternatives considered | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | As built | **Authentication inside the Spring Boot service** instead of the separate NestJS auth service in the plan. | One service to secure, deploy and operate for a five-person team; Spring Security, BCrypt and signed tokens met BR-01–BR-03. | Separate NestJS auth service (planned). | Fewer moving parts; auth can still be extracted later behind the same token contract. |
| D2 | As built | **No Kafka.** Trade, fill, cash and login events are written to an outbox table by database triggers, ready for an event backbone. | Reporting separation (BR-16) is achieved inside Postgres (D11) without running a broker. | Kafka event backbone (planned). | Outbox keeps the option open: a publisher can stream it to Kafka later without changing the trading code. |
| D3 | As built | **MyBatis + Flyway on PostgreSQL.** | Explicit SQL for financial queries; versioned, reviewable schema changes (Objective 4). | JPA/Hibernate. | Every schema change is a numbered migration in source control. |
| D4 | 2026-10-21 | **Orders are priced and executed by the server**, after acceptance, by an execution worker with a recovery sweep. | BR-06 (record intent before execution), BR-08 (price at execution). Previously the browser triggered the fill and chose the price — a client could buy at any price. | Keep client-triggered fills with server validation. | Clients cannot influence prices; execution survives the client closing the page. |
| D5 | 2026-10-21 | **BUY at the ask, SELL at the bid**; **limit orders fill immediately at the limit or better, otherwise are rejected** (no resting orders). | Matches how a client is priced against a quote; resting orders need a matching engine and are not required by BR-04. | Price at last trade; resting limit orders. | Simple, explainable outcomes; resting/stop orders are a future capability. |
| D6 | 2026-10-21 | **One outcome per order** (unique constraint) and **insert-only orders, fills and trade events** (database triggers), matching cash events. | Section 9.1 (no duplicates) and 9.4 (not alterable). | Application-level checks only. | Guarantees hold even with bugs or several API instances. Test data cannot be deleted from these tables. |
| D7 | 2026-10-21 | **Status names: ACCEPTED, FILLED, REJECTED.** "Submitted" is recorded as an audit event; a submission that fails the rules is refused immediately with a reason and creates no order. | BR-07 wording. | Separate SUBMITTED and ACCEPTED states. | One clear status per order on screen; full detail in the audit trail. |
| D8 | 2026-10-21 | **Accounts are USD-only; non-USD prices are converted with live FX rates**, recording local price, currency and rate. | BR-12 adds INR and GBP instruments; multi-currency cash is a larger change with Finance implications. | Multi-currency accounts. | No mispricing across currencies; Finance may later ask for multi-currency cash. |
| D9 | 2026-10-21 | **UK equities via US listings** (Shell, AstraZeneca, HSBC, BP, GSK, Vodafone); LSE listings loaded inactive. | The market-data provider has no London market. | Omit UK; fabricate UK prices. | BR-12 partly met; Sponsor decision needed on a UK data source (risk R1). |
| D10 | 2026-10-21 | **Market hours per market** (crypto 24/7, FX weekday, India IST, UK London, US New York). | Previously everything followed US hours, blocking Indian equities during their session. | Single US session. | Holidays not yet modelled (risk R11). |
| D11 | 2026-10-21 | **Reporting reads analytics facts maintained by a background projector**, not a trigger inside the fill transaction; insights moved off live tables. | BR-16: reporting must not compete with trading. | Synchronous trigger (previous); read replica. | Reports trail trading by ≤10 s; a read replica remains the next step at scale. |
| D12 | 2026-10-21 | **BR-18 capability: market context linked to the client's assets.** | Serves the Joanna persona at the moment of decision, off the trading path. See [`BR-18-differentiation.md`](BR-18-differentiation.md). | Price alerts, stop orders, P&L/cost basis, leaderboard, options. | **Pending Sponsor sign-off.** |
| D13 | 2026-10-21 | **Market depth panel is not live data** (levels are generated around the price); no change made yet. | The provider has no order book. | Show the platform's own order book (needs resting orders, D5); label as simulated; remove. | **Open:** decide before showcase — see risk R13. |
